## Proposal to Purchase the Business and Certain Assets of Highland Computer Forms, Inc.

Dear Rob & Lynne,

This letter will confirm that Wise Business Forms, Inc. (the "Buyer") is interested in acquiring the Business and certain Assets of Highland Computer Forms, Inc. (the "Company"), without assuming any of the Company's Liabilities, on terms that would be mutually agreeable. In this letter, (a) Buyer and the Company are sometimes called singularly a "Party" and collectively the "Parties" or Seller, and (b) Buyer's possible acquisition of certain Assets of the Company is sometimes called the "Possible Acquisition."

The Parties wish to commence immediately the negotiation of a definitive written purchase agreement providing for the Possible Acquisition (a "Purchase Agreement"). To facilitate the negotiation of a Purchase Agreement, the Parties request that Buyer's counsel prepare an initial draft, which would be delivered to Seller not later than September 15, 2026. The execution of any such Purchase Agreement would be subject to the satisfactory completion of Buyer's ongoing investigation of the Company's business and would also be subject to approval by Buyer's board of directors.

Based upon the information currently known to Buyer, it is proposed that the Purchase Agreement include the following:

1. **Transfer of Assets and Liabilities**
   1. **Transfer of Assets and Liabilities.** The Company would sell all of its operating Assets, property, rights, goodwill and business free from all liens and encumbrances, including the trade name "Highland and 1--ICF" to Buyer for Consideration set forth in Paragraph 2 below.
   2. **Excluded Assets.** Cash, Accounts Receivable, Prepaids, and Real Estate.
   3. **Excluded Liabilities.** Buyer would assume none of the Liabilities of the Company, with all Liabilities (the "Excluded Liabilities") being retained by the Company. The Excluded Liabilities would include, but not be limited to, the following: (i) Accounts Payable, (ii) any liability relating to assets or contractual supply commitments to be retained by the Company, (iii) any liability under any employment, severance, retention or termination agreement with any employee of the Company or its affiliates, (iv) any liability relating to employee grievances, or for payroll, vacation, workers' compensation or similar matters or otherwise arising under any employee benefit, stock option, pension, health care, profit-sharing or similar plan or arrangement, (v) any liability for taxes, including taxes arising as a result of the Company's operation of its businesses or the ownership of its assets prior to the closing, (vi) any environmental, health and safety liabilities arising out of or relating to the operation of the Company's business or the Company's leasing, ownership or operation of real property, (vii) any Debt other than any capitalized leases that might be assumed, (viii) any and all Pension-related obligations, and (ix) any other liability of any nature arising from the conduct of Business by the Company prior to the Closing.
   4. **Closing.** The closing of this transaction (the "Closing") would occur simultaneously with the execution of a Purchase Agreement. The Parties intend that the Closing would occur on or before October 30, 2026, subject to the successful negotiation and execution of a Purchase Agreement and the satisfaction of all closing conditions thereunder.

2. **Consideration**

   Total Consideration shall be based on both the Company's September 30, 2026 annualized internal Balance Sheet/Income Statement, FYE 2025 statements, and its final FYE 2025 CPA reviewed statements. The Purchase Price on a "Cash-free/Debt-free" basis would be based on a valuation of 3.5 times the Company's Adjusted EBITDA that is agreed on by Buyer and Company and would contain the following components:

   1. The deal is contingent on a 3-5 year contract with Pixelle being executed.
   2. FIVE HUNDRED THOUSAND DOLLARS ($500,000) to be paid at closing.
   3. Payment for all Raw Material Inventory (valued at original cost net of rebates, discounts, and obsolescence) not to exceed ONE MILLION DOLLARS ($1,000,000) to be paid at closing for all Raw Material Inventory (estimated $1,000,000 net of rebates and discounts). If the value is $1,100,000, Buyer still pays a maximum of $1,000,000. If the value is $900,000 then Buyer pays $900,000. Raw material inventory is assumed to consist mostly of paper but also includes all raw materials and supplies needed to run the business, including but not limited to cartons, carbon, envelopes, ink, plates, pallets, etc.
   4. Finished Goods Inventory to be paid for monthly at the original invoiced price as used by Buyer post-Closing after Buyer ships such items of Finished Goods Inventory, invoices the customer for such items, and the customer pays Buyer; any customer returns or remakes related to pre-transaction work will be deducted from future earnouts.
   5. Remaining amount paid based on contingent Earn Out equal to THREE to FOUR PERCENT (3.0-4.0%) (determined as a result of the actual agreed-upon annualized calculation of the EBITDA) of the annual net sales (meaning sales less freight revenue, rebates) from repeat orders of the Business after the Closing for a period of five (5) years after the close of transaction, to be paid quarterly for most recently completed quarter.
   6. Wise takes ownership of Angola equipment and will remove equipment from the Angola facility but is not responsible to get it in a ready-for-sale condition.
   7. Wise will rent 1025 West Main Street, Hillsboro, OH 45133. Base rent for the 75,957 sq. ft. facility would initially be $2.00 per ft. NNN for the balance of 2026 through December 2027 and would be increased by 3% on each anniversary of the lease. Amounts different than current run rate will be included in adjusted EBITDA calculation.
   8. Wise will rent Hillsboro #2 facility with base rent for the 15,000 sq. ft. facility at a fixed rate of $2.70 per ft. NNN on a month-to-month basis for as long as Pixelle business is contracted and would be increased by 3% each year starting January 2028. Amounts different than current run rate will be included in adjusted EBITDA calculation.
   9. $1.00 for the Forms-related customer list and all art and paper files of Highland Computer Forms, Inc., as well as data from all software.

3. **Employee Matters; Transition Services**

   Buyer does not intend to make an offer of employment to any of the Company's current employees of Angola, IN except to Rob Jones. Wise may choose to offer employment to some or all Angola employees in its Ft. Wayne, IN plant. Any and all severance payments and other payments that become due to Angola employees of Company or others as a result of the termination of employment with the Company shall constitute Excluded Liabilities for which the Company shall be solely responsible. Wise will offer employment to Hillsboro employees and any accrued benefits up to acquisition date will be paid by Company.

4. **Other Terms and Conditions**

   The Company, Rob Jones and Lynne Patton would make customary representations and warranties to Buyer and would provide customary covenants against competition, other covenants, indemnifications, and other protections for the benefit of Buyer. The consummation of the contemplated transactions by Buyer would be subject to the satisfaction of various conditions, including but not limited to:

   1. Buyer's satisfaction, in sole discretion, with the results of the legal, accounting and business due diligence investigation performed by its attorneys, accountants and representatives.
   2. Receipt of all necessary consents and approvals of governmental bodies, lenders, lessors, and other third parties.
   3. Absence of any material adverse change in the Company's business, financial condition, prospects, assets, or operations since June 30, 2026; absence of pending or threatened litigation regarding the Purchase Agreement or the transactions to be contemplated thereby; and delivery of customary legal opinions, closing certificates, and other documentation.
   4. Wise will assume responsibility only for the lease(s) related to specific leased assets identified on the lease asset transition list in the Purchase Agreement and the specifically identified lease(s) in the Purchase Agreement.

The following paragraphs of this letter (the "Binding Provisions") are the legally binding and enforceable agreements of Buyer and the Company.

5. **Access**

   During the period from the date this letter is signed on behalf of the Company (the "Signing Date") until the date on which either Party provides the other Party with written notice that negotiations toward a Purchase Agreement are terminated (the "Termination Date") and with prior permission, the Company will afford Buyer full and free access to the Company, its personnel, customers, suppliers, properties, contracts, books and records and all other documents and data, notwithstanding the confidentiality provisions referred to in Paragraph 8 of this letter.

6. **Exclusivity**

   The Company will not, directly or indirectly, through any representative or otherwise, solicit, consider, entertain, discuss, negotiate or accept any offer or proposal of any other person relating to the acquisition of the Company, its capital stock, assets or business, in whole or in part, whether directly or indirectly, through purchase, merger, consolidation or otherwise (other than sales of inventory in the ordinary course) or enter into any letter of intent or other agreement relating thereto with any person other than Buyer.

7. **Conduct of Business**

   During the period from the Signing Date until the Termination Date, the Company shall notify Buyer immediately of (a) any conduct of the Company outside of the ordinary course of business and of any extraordinary transactions proposed to be engaged in by the Company, (b) any payment or commitment to make any material capital expenditures by the Company, (c) any knowledge of or anticipation of an expected change in customer revenue or relationship, and (d) maintain roll paper inventory level at the lowest possible level not to exceed.

8. **Confidential Information**

   Except as expressly modified by the Binding Provisions, the Confidentiality Agreement executed by Buyer on or about July 2026 shall remain in full force and effect.

9. **Disclosure**

   Except as and to the extent required by law, without the prior written consent of the other Party, neither the Buyer nor the Company will make, directly or indirectly, any public comment, statement or communication with respect to, or otherwise to disclose or to permit the disclosure of the existence of discussions regarding, a possible transaction between the Parties or any of the terms, conditions or other aspects of the transaction proposed in this letter. If a Party is required by law to make any such disclosure, it must first provide to the other Party the content of the proposed disclosure, the reasons that such disclosure is required by law, and the time and place that the disclosure will be made.

10. **Cost and Expenses**

    Buyer and the Company will be responsible for and bear all of their respective costs and expenses (including any broker's or finder's fees and the expenses of its representatives) incurred at any time in connection with pursuing or consummating the Possible Acquisition. It is understood that any fee that might be due Corporate Development Associates shall be the sole responsibility of the Company and will be paid at closing.

11. **Cooperation**

    During the period from the Signing Date until the Termination Date, Buyer and the Company will cooperate with each other and proceed, as promptly as is reasonably practical, to seek to obtain all necessary consents and approvals from lenders, landlords, and other third parties, and to endeavor to comply with all other legal or contractual requirements for or preconditions to the execution and consummation of the Purchase Agreement.

12. **Entire Agreement; Amendment or Modification**

    The Binding Provisions contained in Paragraphs 5-16 constitute the entire agreement between the Parties and supersede all prior oral or written agreements, understandings, representations and warranties and courses of conduct and dealing between the Parties on the subject matter thereof. Except as otherwise provided herein, the Binding Provisions may be amended or modified only by a writing executed by all of the Parties.

13. **Governing Law**

    The Binding Provisions will be governed by and construed under the laws of the State of Georgia without regard to conflicts-of-laws principles.

14. **Termination**

    The Binding Provisions may be terminated earlier upon written notice by either Party to the other Party unilaterally, for any reason or no reason, with or without cause, at any time. Upon termination of the Binding Provisions, the Parties will have no further obligations hereunder, except with respect to Paragraphs 8 and 10 above.

15. **Counterparts**

    This letter may be executed in one or more counterparts, each of which will be deemed to be an original of this letter and all of which, when taken together, will be deemed to constitute one and the same letter.

16. **Binding Effect**

    The provisions of Paragraphs 1 through 4 of this letter are intended only as an expression of intent on behalf of Buyer, are not intended to be legally binding on Buyer or the Company and are expressly subject to the execution of an appropriate Purchase Agreement. Moreover, except as expressly provided in Paragraphs 5 through 16 (or as expressly provided in any binding written agreement that the Parties may enter into in the future), no past or future action, course of conduct or failure to act relating to the Possible Acquisition, or relating to the negotiation of the terms of the Possible Acquisition or any Purchase Agreement, will give rise to or serve as a basis for any obligation or other liability on the part of Buyer or the Company.
