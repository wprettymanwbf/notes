# Explanation of Basic Rules – Carbon Interleaf

_Source: January 1991 Wise Price Book, PDF page 11 (printed page 11)_

Wise is a short and medium run plant with roll to sheet and roll to roll presses. We are a camera ready plant, however, we do have complete typesetting facilities available. Our normal delivery time will be 10 working days.

Our short run prices are based on 1000 minimum quantity. Our medium run prices are based on a minimum of 10,000 - 8 1/2" stubs, 15,000 - 5 2/3" stubs, 20,000 - 4 1/4" stubs and 25,000 - 3.4" stubs. Our short run maximum roll width is 12 1/4", which will give you an 11 3/4" job after trim. Our roll to sheet presses consist of three offset units and one marginal and numbering unit.

Combinations:
Two or more lots of the same size entered at the same time for the same customer with a maximum of 3 orders allowed in one combination. Must all be priced from the same pricing list (either all short run quantity/or all medium run quantity).

We reserve the right to turn down any job that we feel does not fit our presses. We cannot run over a five part form, without upcharge. We cannot collate over 8 parts on roll-to-roll. Our medium run maximum roll width is 15 1/2".

Our trade customs are those of the National Business Forms Association. All jobs F.O.B. factory.

All printing done Litho. Proofs given-one set and additional sets at $5.00 per leaf or burn.

We offer a 3/4" stub and/or with line holes and our standard carbon is 1/2" shorter at the opposite end of the stub. If your medium run snap apart cannot have line holes in the stub area, there will be a $7.50 flat charge to run your job with a false stub and trim to eliminate line holes. Any deviations, check special pricing.

Snap-aparts are micr'd off line on a Miehle Vertical Letterpress. Consecutive and static micr'ing available.

Cancellations. Orders are subject to cancellation and all work completed up to the date of cancellation will be billed, with a minimum of $10.00. Such charges are net and not subject to discount.

Basic Printing Charge: The following items are included in your basic printing charge. One roll in the press, one base plate (front copy) black ink and the standard 3/4" stub. Note: numbering, punching, roll changes, marginals, etc. will be charged for. We shrink wrap all snap-aparts.

Dealer Trade Discount: 15% discount on all list prices.

**For additional rules and further general information, see inside front cover.**
