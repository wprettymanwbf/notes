Explanation of Basic Rules – Continuous

Source: January 1991 Wise Price Book, PDF page 2

I. General Information

  • A. Trade and Manufacturer's Policies

    Wise Business Forms subscribes to accepted Business Forms Industry Trade Customs and Practices as published by the National Business Forms Association.

    All orders are subject to approval by the plant and may be cancelled only by agreement with the plant. Prices, conditions and specifications are subject to change without notice.

  • B. Wise Business Forms is a short and medium run plant. Our prices are based on camera ready copy. However, we do have complete typesetting facilities available.

    1. Policies Pertaining to Orders

      All orders must include clear and complete copy, construction specifications and forms usage. Printed copy should be submitted when possible. Manuscript copy must include exact spacing measurements on a ruled spacing chart. No orders will be entered or scheduled until all instructions, materials and specifications have been received by the plant.

      • a. Orders and correspondence should be addressed and mailed to Wise Business Forms, Inc., Box 1666, Butler, PA 16003 or Box 8550, Fort Wayne, IN 46898, or 555 McFarland 400 Dr., Alpharetta, GA 30201.

      • b. Overrun and underrun. Orders are subject to an overrun or underrun of up to 10%.

      • c. Acknowledgements. All orders for custom printed forms will be acknowledged by the plant. Wise factory order number and scheduled proof or shipping date are shown in upper right corner of specifications. It is the responsibility of the distributor to check specifications against the original order to insure correctness of plant interpretation.

        Orders received on National Business Forms Association Standard Order Forms will be accepted and Wise will produce forms as specified on those forms.

      • d. Prices, Imprint, Shipments. Prices shown in price schedules are list. We will imprint the distributor's name in the stub or marginal perf area if the art or photocopy includes it or if requested by the distributor. Unless otherwise requested, Wise will ship in the name of the distributor to the distributor's customer.

      • e. Deliveries. Every effort will be made by the plant to ship orders on or before the acknowledged shipping date. The plant cannot guarantee delivery on any specific date, and it is not liable in any way for failure to comply with any predicted delivery.

      • f. Cancellations. Orders are subject to cancellation and all work completed up to the date of cancellation will be billed, with a minimum of $10.00. Such charges are net and not subject to discount.

      • g. Complaints. All complaints, requests for return, rework or adjustment must be made to the Customer Service Department. No merchandise can be returned to the plant for any reason without prior authorization from the Customer Service Department.

    2. Distributor Credit

      Prior approval by the Credit Department is required for entering an order. Credit applications available upon request.

    3. Special Pricing or Factory Estimates

      Special pricing or factory estimates are valid for a period of 60 days from date quoted unless otherwise stated in writing. Contact Customer Service Department.

II. Discounts

  1. Trade: 15%
  2. Open Account: 3%-15 days, Net 30

III. Basic Forms Cost

Flat charges to be divided by lowest full 500 multiples.

  • A. PAPER

    Basic Forms Costs include 12# or 15# white. All parts same weight.

    1. Paper roll changes. Charge for each change in size, grade, weight, or color.
    2. For papers other than shown as part of basic forms cost, see specific price schedules.
    3. We stock the standard roll widths. 8 1/2, 10, 12 1/2, and 15 1/2 for register bond. 1/4" needed for press trim. 8 1/4, 9 3/4, 12 1/4 and 15 1/4 for carbonless stock.

  • B. CARBON

    1. Basic Forms Costs include standard black or blue (7 1/2# tissue). For continuous forms carbons are narrow 3/8" right and left when crimped. If form glues, carbon is usually slit for better carbon removal and $7.50 carbon slit charge will apply.
    2. Stripe or pattern carbons require a flat charge and running charge for each variation in color, grade, pattern or stripe. Consult factory.

  • C. INK

    1. Wise standard black or color included in basic forms cost at no charge.

    2. Wise Standard ink colors are as follows:

      Color PMS
      Black PMS Black
      Gray PMS 423, 415
      Red PMS 185, Warm Red
      Dark Green PMS 348
      Bright Green PMS 354
      Light Blue PMS Process Blue
      Dark Blue PMS Reflex Blue
      Brown PMS 471, 161
      Orange PMS 151
      Yellow PMS Yellow

    3. PMS matched ink colors are available at a flat up-charge.

    4. Commercial Match is available at $10.00.

    5. Multiple color printing. Two, three, and four-color printing is available on face or back, (total of four colors). Consult summary of extra charges for two, three, or four-color printing, in addition to ink color charges. Four-color printing on 14" and 22" press (available at Butler Plant only).

    6. Composition charges for each color are priced separately.

    Magnetic Ink

    • a. Static and consecutive magnetic ink applied off line with a Mark V Encoder. Our MICR is crash imprinted. Interior live micr available (consult factory). Mod 9 also available on continuous forms only. Our consecutive MICR charge includes static portion.

  • D. COMPOSITION

    1. Photo Copy. Wise reserves the right to determine if camera-ready paste-up repro proofs, glossies, original artwork or negatives are useable. These will be used, and Flat-Photo base charges used, if the following conditions are met.

      • a. Camera copy, artwork or negatives are not folded, stapled or otherwise mutilated and are supplied exact size of finished form.
      • b. Camera copy is printed in dark ink, preferably black or red, on white paper free of smudges or other extraneous marks, and has no screens.
      • c. Camera copy, artwork or negatives include 7/16" lock-up space parallel to between sets perf.
      • d. No changes are required from camera copy, artwork or negatives submitted.
      • e. Separate camera copy, artwork or negatives must be submitted for each color on each part and each part-to-part composition change and must include accurate registration marks. Screens cannot be photographed. Negatives are to be furnished unmasked.

    2. Back Printing

      Composition change charges apply to back printing, as well as face.

    3. Marginal Words and Designating Numerals. Standard marginal words and numerals are set in 10-point type on one line not to exceed 5" in length and printed in red ink.

    4. Proofs

      • a. One set of proofs will be submitted for approval if requested or if required by the plant for clarification. Proofs are scheduled to be mailed within 10 working days from date of order entry by Wise. No charge is made for one proof, $5.00 flat net for each additional proof burn. Acetate proofs can be provided upon request at a charge of $5.75 each burn. Color key available (consult factory).
      • b. Changes on proofs, author's alterations, other than minimal corrections such as typographical errors, will be charged at cost.
      • c. Delivery schedule and ordering of materials will not commence until receipt of final signed proof by the plant unless prior written authorization to order materials has been received by the plant. Telephone approvals allowed only on xerox or fax proofs.
      • d. Each part of face and back printing must be initialled or signed for proper approval of proof. The responsibility of correctness of the proof, on signing, rests with the customer.

  • E. REORDER ALLOWANCE

    1. On exact repeat orders, deduct $12.50 flat and any screen charges. Maximum 3 years old.

  • F. CONSTRUCTION

    1. Standard fastening for continuous forms consist of one crimp every 2" in one or both margins.
    2. Gluing available at no up-charge in 1 or both margins.

  • G. COMBINED ORDERS

    Two or more forms of the same length and the same width entered at the same time and for the same customer may be combined for pricing purposes, provided they can be run and shipped at the same time.

    For pricing, use one basic flat charge for the basic form plus $19.25 flat for each order placed in combination. Also add any necessary plate changes between forms or parts. Use appropriate flat and running charges for each form according to number of parts, ink and paper changes, etc.

    1. Minimum quantity of each form entered must be 1,000.
    2. Maximum orders allowed in one combination: 3.
    3. Prefix and Suffix changes, minimum or multiples of 1M sets.
    4. Reset numbers, minimum or multiples 1M sets.
    5. Change in marginal words or designating numerals, minimum or multiples 1M sets.

  • H. NUMBERING

    1. Press numbering. Contact customer Service Department for two or more numbers on face or back of form. A list of missing numbers, if any, will be furnished. If other than red ink numbering is required, there is a $13.50 wash-up charge.
    2. Crash numbering. Available only on multi-part checks. Numbers are in red ink on original only and carbon impressions on other parts. On carbon interleaved sets, number must be over carbon area. Wise can guarantee no missing numbers on crash number at a $28.00 flat upcharge. A list of missing numbers, if any, will be supplied.

  • I. PERFORATIONS

    Charge as shown for each perforation position and for each change from part to part. Standard marginal perforations are 1/2" either or both sides. Charge for any deviation.

  • J. PUNCHING

    Press punching through some or all of paper parts. Charges are for each set-up of punching. Charge for each change in punching position or size of hole. Collator punching through all paper and carbon parts not available.

  • K. PACKAGING AND LABELING

    1. Packaging
      • a. Standard size forms up to 14-7/8" x 11" are packed in press-seal cartons 12" high.
    2. Labeling
      • a. Wise provides blank labels on cartons, showing distributor name and address on return portion. No other name shown on carton, labels or forms unless specified on original order.
      • b. Distributor shipping labels will be used if supplied with order.
      • c. Breaks in cartons are labeled on outside of such cartons.

  • L. TRANSPORTATION

    1. All orders are shipped F.O.B. factory.
    2. Shipments will be made best way, usually U.P.S. or by truck, unless otherwise specified on the order.
