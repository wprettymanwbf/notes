# Wise — Chief Executive Officer

*Job Description and Compensation Plan*

**Company:** ~$50M commercial printing operation — business forms, labels, and packaging — with five manufacturing plants.

**Purpose:** Own enterprise results and decide where Wise puts its capital and its capacity.

**Reports to:** Board / ownership
**Direct reports:** VP of Sales, VP of Operations, VP of Finance, HR lead

---

## Owns

- Enterprise P&L, strategy, and annual operating plan
- Capital allocation across five plants and the label/packaging build
- Plant footprint decisions — specialization, consolidation, closure
- M&A: sourcing, diligence, and integration of tuck-in acquisitions
- Executive team selection, development, and accountability
- Board and lender relationships
- Pricing exceptions above delegated authority

## Measures

- EBITDA vs. plan
- Revenue growth, with label and packaging broken out
- Cash conversion / return on invested capital
- Integration milestones
- Leadership bench depth and succession coverage

---

## Compensation

| | |
|---|---|
| **Base salary** | $275,000–350,000 |
| **Target bonus** | 40% of base |
| **Maximum bonus** | 80% of base |
| **Target total cash** | $385,000–490,000 |

Position within range by experience, prior scale, and geography.

### Annual bonus scorecard

| Measure | Weight |
|---|---|
| EBITDA vs. plan | 50% |
| Revenue growth, label & packaging broken out | 25% |
| Cash conversion / ROIC | 15% |
| Strategic milestones — integration, leadership bench | 10% |

### Long-term incentive

Three-year phantom equity or cash-settled appreciation plan, sized at roughly 3–8% of equity value, vesting on a rolling three-year cycle and settling on a defined valuation formula or a liquidity event. Tie 60% to cumulative EBITDA and return on invested capital.

### Benchmark basis

Chief Executive Research, *CEO and Senior Executive Compensation in Private Companies 2024-25* (1,534 US private companies surveyed March–July 2024): median private-company CEO 2024 expected cash compensation was $325,000 base plus $100,000 bonus. CEO pay scales sharply with revenue — median total compensation was $315,500 at $10–24.9M revenue and $608,364 at $100–249.9M, interpolating to roughly $400–475K at $50M. Ranges above sit at or modestly below survey median, since the survey is all-industry and commercial printing pays below the all-industry median.
