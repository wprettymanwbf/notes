# Wise — VP of Finance

*Job Description and Compensation Plan*

**Company:** ~$50M commercial printing operation — business forms, labels, and packaging — with five manufacturing plants.

**Purpose:** Close the books fast and clean, protect cash and margin, and make the numbers usable for decisions.

**Reports to:** CEO
**Direct reports:** Accounting, FP&A, credit and collections, IT

---

## Owns

- Monthly close, financial reporting, and the audit/review relationship
- Budget, forecast, and the annual operating plan mechanics
- Treasury, banking, debt covenants, and cash forecasting
- Credit, collections, and DSO
- Cost model, standard costs, margin floors, and price-list governance
- Capital request process: discipline, hurdle rates, post-investment review
- Plant-level and product-level profitability reporting
- IT systems and the ERP/MIS roadmap
- M&A diligence and purchase accounting support
- Insurance and risk

## Does not own

- **Pricing to market** — VP of Sales, who prices within the floors Finance sets
- **Estimating** — VP of Operations, who owns the manufacturing cost calculation

Finance sets and governs margin policy; it does not negotiate individual prices.

## Measures

- EBITDA vs. plan
- Days to close; forecast accuracy vs. actual
- DSO and working capital
- Covenant compliance and liquidity headroom
- Clean audit / no material control findings
- Margin governance: realization reporting and exception discipline

---

## Compensation

| | |
|---|---|
| **Base salary** | $180,000–225,000 |
| **Target bonus** | 25% of base |
| **Maximum bonus** | 50% of base |
| **Target total cash** | $225,000–281,000 |

Position within range by experience, prior scale, and geography.

### Annual bonus scorecard

| Measure | Weight |
|---|---|
| EBITDA vs. plan | 40% |
| Cash — DSO and working capital | 25% |
| Close timeliness, forecast accuracy, clean controls | 20% |
| Margin and capital governance | 15% |

### Long-term incentive

Three-year phantom equity or cash-settled appreciation plan, sized at roughly 0.5–2.0% of equity value, vesting on a rolling three-year cycle and settling on a defined valuation formula or a liquidity event. Tie 60% to cumulative EBITDA and return on invested capital.

### Benchmark basis

Chief Executive Research, *CEO and Senior Executive Compensation in Private Companies 2024-25* (1,534 US private companies surveyed March–July 2024): median private-company CFO compensation was $207,500 base plus $40,000 bonus, $273,575 total. Ranges above sit at or modestly below survey median, since the survey is all-industry and skews to companies larger than $50M, and commercial printing pays below the all-industry median.
