# Wise — VP of Sales

*Job Description and Compensation Plan*

**Company:** ~$50M commercial printing operation — business forms, labels, and packaging — with five manufacturing plants.

**Purpose:** Grow revenue and margin, and shift the mix toward label and packaging.

**Reports to:** CEO
**Direct reports:** Outside sales, national/channel accounts, marketing, sales operations

---

## Owns

- Revenue vs. plan and the sales plan by segment
- Channel strategy — distributor/trade vs. direct, and the mix between them
- Sales force: hiring, territories, quotas, development, performance management
- Pricing to market within approved margin floors
- Label and packaging revenue development, working against Operations' capability roadmap
- Marketing, samples, trade shows, and the CRM/pipeline system
- Customer retention and win-back on the repeat-revenue base

## Does not own

- **Customer service and order entry** — VP of Operations. CSRs sit at the plants next to scheduling; the role is production coordination, not account growth.
- **Estimating** — VP of Operations. An estimate is a manufacturing calculation.
- **Margin floors** — VP of Finance. Sales prices to market within them.

## Measures

- Revenue vs. plan
- Gross margin dollars and price realization vs. floor
- Label and packaging revenue growth vs. plan
- New account acquisition and customer retention
- Rep productivity and quota attainment

---

## Compensation

| | |
|---|---|
| **Base salary** | $170,000–215,000 |
| **Target bonus** | 35% of base |
| **Maximum bonus** | 70% of base |
| **Target total cash** | $230,000–290,000 |

Position within range by experience, prior scale, and geography.

### Annual bonus scorecard

| Measure | Weight |
|---|---|
| Revenue vs. plan | 40% |
| Gross margin dollars / price realization vs. floor | 30% |
| Label & packaging revenue growth vs. plan | 20% |
| Retention and new account acquisition | 10% |

Margin realization is weighted heavily on purpose: it stops the plan from rewarding revenue bought with price.

### Long-term incentive

Three-year phantom equity or cash-settled appreciation plan, sized at roughly 0.5–2.0% of equity value, vesting on a rolling three-year cycle and settling on a defined valuation formula or a liquidity event. Tie 60% to cumulative EBITDA and return on invested capital.

### Benchmark basis

Chief Executive Research, *CEO and Senior Executive Compensation in Private Companies 2024-25* (1,534 US private companies surveyed March–July 2024): median private-company Chief Sales Officer compensation was $192,250 base plus $50,250 bonus, $268,575 total. Ranges above sit at or modestly below survey median, since the survey is all-industry and skews to companies larger than $50M, and commercial printing pays below the all-industry median.
